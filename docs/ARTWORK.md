# Artwork and screenshot provenance

The contour field, renderer, icon, TV banner, and DreamService preview are original to this repository. No photos, video, third-party fonts, or external visual assets are bundled.

`docs/screenshots/contour-flow-google-tv-api34-1920x1080.png` is an unmodified ADB capture of `PreviewActivity` on the `aql-googletv34` Google TV emulator. It shows the Glacier palette with Day lighting after the scene ran for more than 45 seconds. `docs/screenshots/contour-flow-settings-google-tv-api34-1920x1080.png` shows the settings screen on the same emulator. Both images were opened and visually inspected. `art/screensaver-preview.jpg` is a 384 by 216 resize of the scene capture for ecosystem thumbnails. The small in-app DreamService preview is the original vector resource in `res/drawable/preview.xml`.

These emulator images establish preview rendering at the emulator's configured 1920 by 1080 resolution. They do not establish DreamService activation on a TV, physical display resolution, native 4K output, or sustained thermal behavior.
